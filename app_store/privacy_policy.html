MD Fit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

MD Fit (“we”, “our”, “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the control you have over it. It applies to the MD Fit apps for iPhone and Android and to the MD Fit website.

MD Fit is an educational movement platform. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and using it does not create a physician–patient relationship.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address
  • Name (optional)
  • Password, stored only as a salted hash — never in readable form
  • If you use Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google, we receive the identifier and email address that provider releases to us, and nothing else

Usage data

  • Workouts completed, programs started and resumed
  • Videos viewed and exercise preferences
  • Streaks, achievements and progress totals

Device information

  • Device type, operating system version and app version
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can deliver reminders
  • Crash and performance diagnostics, which are not tied to your identity

Payments

Memberships and program purchases are processed by Stripe. Card numbers are entered on Stripe’s own checkout and never reach MD Fit servers. We store only whether a membership or purchase is active, and its history.

What we do not collect

  • Protected Health Information (PHI)
  • Medical diagnoses or clinical records from the app
  • Health insurance information
  • Social Security numbers
  • Location data
  • Apple Health or Google Fit data
  • Advertising identifiers — we run no ads and do no cross-app tracking

How we use your information

  • To provide the app: your account, library access, programs and saved progress
  • To track workouts, streaks and achievements you asked us to track
  • To send notifications you enabled — visit reminders, messages from the practice and streak nudges. Notifications never include health details.
  • To process memberships and purchases
  • To understand aggregate app performance and fix problems

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that make the app work — payment processing, video hosting, email delivery, push notification delivery and application hosting — and only to the extent each needs to perform that task. We may disclose information where required by law.

Data storage and security

Data is stored on managed cloud infrastructure and is encrypted in transit using industry standard TLS. Access is limited to the people who operate the service. Passwords are hashed.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account. You can delete your account, and the data associated with it, from inside the app under Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also email us and we will complete the deletion for you.
  • Request a copy of your data. Email us and we will provide it.
  • Correct your information. Update your name and email in your profile.
  • Turn off notifications. In the app, or in your device settings.
  • Opt out of marketing email. Use the unsubscribe link in any message.

Deleting your account removes your profile, progress and preferences. Records we must keep for legal, tax or accounting reasons — such as payment records — are retained as the law requires.

Children

MD Fit is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
