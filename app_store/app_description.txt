Movement. The true medicine.

MD Fit is the movement-education app from Dr. Jared Caban's practice in Sarasota, Florida. Find out what tends to refer pain to the spot that hurts, learn movements used for that area, and build the habit with structured programs and progress you can actually see.

PAIN TOOL
Tap a body region on the map. See the referred-pain patterns associated with it, then the movements commonly used for that area — each one filmed and explained. Every result carries plain guidance on when to stop and when to see a professional in person.

VIDEO LIBRARY
Professionally filmed exercise videos covering mobility and stretching, strength with proper form, corrective and rehab movement, and activation and warm-up sequences. Search and filter by region, equipment and level.

WORKOUT GENERATOR
Build a session around what you have today. Choose focus, difficulty, length, rounds and rest, add a warm-up, cool-down or core work, and get a full workout you can start immediately or schedule for later.

PROGRAMS
Structured multi-week training built around a sport or a goal: golf, pickleball, running, skiing, general mobility, strength and conditioning. Pick up where you left off, on any device.

PROGRESS
Streaks, achievements and a calendar of what you have done. Reminders arrive when you asked for them, and nowhere else.

FOR PATIENTS OF THE PRACTICE
Book visits, see upcoming appointments, message the practice and keep the plan Dr. Caban gave you in the same place as the movements themselves.

FREE TO START
The Pain Tool and a daily selection of videos are free. A membership unlocks the full library, every program and the workout generator without limits.

MD Fit is educational. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and using it does not create a physician-patient relationship. If your symptoms are severe, worsening or unexplained, see a qualified professional.
