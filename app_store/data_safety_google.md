# Google Play Data safety answers (Policy -> App content -> Data safety)

Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? -> YES
Is all user data encrypted in transit?                             -> YES
Do you provide a way for users to request data deletion?            -> YES (in-app: Profile -> Account -> Delete account, and by email)
Has your app been independently validated against a security standard? -> NO

## Data types collected

Personal info
- Name          Collected, not shared. Required. Purpose: Account management, App functionality.
- Email address Collected, not shared. Required. Purpose: Account management, App functionality.

App activity
- App interactions Collected, not shared. Optional. Purpose: Analytics, App functionality.

Financial info
- Purchase history Collected, not shared. Optional. Purpose: App functionality.
  (Payment card details are collected and processed by Stripe, not by MD Fit.)

App info and performance
- Crash logs        Collected, not shared. Optional. Purpose: App functionality.
- Diagnostics       Collected, not shared. Optional. Purpose: App functionality.

## Declare NOT collected
Health and fitness data, Location, Contacts, Photos and videos, Files and docs,
Calendar, Messages, Audio, Device or other IDs for advertising.

## Health apps declaration
Category: fitness and wellness. NOT a medical device. Does not diagnose or treat.
