# MD Fit Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

## Overview

MD Fit ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
- Email address
- Name (optional)
- Password (encrypted)

**Usage Data**
- Workouts completed
- Exercise preferences
- App usage statistics

**Device Information**
- Device type
- Operating system version
- App version

## Information We Do NOT Collect

- Protected Health Information (PHI)
- Medical diagnoses
- Health insurance information
- Social Security numbers
- Location data

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and improve the app experience
- Track your workout progress and streaks
- Send workout reminders (if enabled)
- Analyze app performance

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Your Rights

You may:
- Request a copy of your data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Opt out of marketing communications
